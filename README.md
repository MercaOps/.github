## MercaOps

Free, private browser tools for online sellers and bookkeepers. Files are processed on your own
device and never uploaded. Every tool works in English and Spanish.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Label Cropper](https://mercaops.com/label-cropper/)** · [ES](https://mercaops.com/es/recortar-etiquetas/) | Crops A4 and Letter shipping labels from Vinted, eBay, Poshmark, Depop, UPS, DHL and more into 4×6 PDFs for thermal printers. |
| **[CSV Cleaner](https://mercaops.com/csv-cleaner/)** · [ES](https://mercaops.com/es/limpiar-csv-bancario/) | Turns bank and payment exports (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, Amex, Bancolombia…) into files QuickBooks, Xero, YNAB or Excel import on the first try. [Pro](https://mercaops.com/pro/) adds QuickBooks .QBO/.IIF and Alegra files. |
| **[Import Calculator](https://mercaops.com/import-calculator/)** · [ES](https://mercaops.com/es/calculadora-importacion/) | Landed cost per unit for cross-border sellers: duty, VAT/IVA, fees and freight by volumetric weight, with the de minimis rules of 16 shipping corridors. |

### Open data

The rules behind the tools, published under CC BY 4.0:

- **[customs-rules-2026](https://github.com/mercaops/customs-rules-2026)**: de minimis, duty and
  VAT rules per corridor, with the official sources and the month each was last verified.
- **[bank-csv-quirks](https://github.com/mercaops/bank-csv-quirks)**: how real bank and payment
  exports are laid out, plus field notes on writing QuickBooks .QBO and .IIF files.

Found an outdated rule or a bank export that changed? Open an issue on the dataset, or write to
[contact@mercaops.com](mailto:contact@mercaops.com).
